The gap absorbs small signal changes. An unknown or disconnected phone never counts as departure.

Unlock requires at least two fresh Near readings inside the filter window for the confirmation time; a single reading never unlocks. Weak readings restart confirmation. This preset restores the default unlock timings (2 s window, 1 s confirmation); Save rules to apply.

Defaults: 2 s unlock window, 1 s unlock confirmation, 6 s departure window, 2 s lock confirmation, 10 s cooldown. Every decision needs at least two readings in its zone. The car's confirmation runs in the background and never delays the next decision.

After a proximity unlock, an automatic lock waits until a door opens or the relock grace passes (3–30 s). After a confirmed proximity lock, a lone keyfob detection within the quiet period (3–30 s) is not an arrival. Normal mode uses 10 s for both.
