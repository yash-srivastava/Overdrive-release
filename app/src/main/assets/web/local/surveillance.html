Operating Mode
Whether Overdrive keeps running after the car is turned off. On Only fully shuts Overdrive down when you park; while-driving features (dashcam, trips, charging) are unaffected.
Overdrive keeps watching while parked — surveillance and background monitoring stay active. Uses a little battery overnight.
These settings control surveillance after the car is off. In On Only mode Overdrive shuts down when parked, so they don't apply. Switch to On & Off to use them.
Arm Mode
When surveillance arms after the car powers off. Both modes honor safe locations and schedules.
Arms when you lock the doors and disarms when you unlock (so it won't record you). If your car can't report lock status, it arms 60s after power-off as a fallback.
ACC-OFF Mode
Smart records motion-triggered events. Continuous records the whole time the car is parked, with no filters and no AI.
Motion + AI gating. Recordings begin only when something moves and AI confirms a person, vehicle, or other relevant object.
Keep USB powered while parked
Keeps the USB ports powered after the car is switched off (for example to charge a phone or power an accessory). Turn off to let the head unit sleep and save the 12V battery. Note: the SD-card slot shares the USB power rail, so turning this off also powers down the SD card while parked — surveillance will record to Internal storage instead. Takes effect the next time the car is switched off.
This mode can keep Android awake, but parked USB and SD-card power remains best-effort because the vehicle firmware controls the physical rail.
Keep mobile data awake while parked
On some models the mobile data module goes to sleep a while after the car is switched off, so the car drops off the network while parked. Turn this on to keep it awake. It is recommended to leave this off unless you actually lose cellular while parked, because keeping the connection alive uses extra battery and mobile data. Only takes effect if mobile data is already on when you park. Takes effect the next time the car is switched off.
Keep Bluetooth on while parked
Shared with Proximity, Automations and key bindings. Saves immediately, independently of surveillance and proximity monitoring. Turning this off disables Bluetooth while vehicle power is off.
Experimental: Keep DI5 awake using BYD Cloud
While ACC is off, requests vehicle status through BYD Cloud every 15 seconds. Requires a connected BYD account and internet access. It may use vehicle SIM data, prevent deep sleep, and increase parked battery use. It stops on ACC-on or when the daemon stops.
Connect and verify your BYD Cloud account before enabling this option.
DiLink 5 parked keep-alive
For DiLink 5 head units, with any camera mode. Helps keep the head unit and vehicle systems awake while parked, without BYD Cloud or internet access. Uses more 12V battery and depends on the vehicle firmware; verify parked ADB and camera availability on your vehicle. Stops below the voltage cutoff and stays off until the car is switched on or charging is detected.
DiLink 5 (Sealion 7): turn driver-assistance features off at every ignition
Child presence detection off at every ignition
For cars with no cabin-camera CPD switch, such as the Sealion 7 model: the DiPilot CPD setting is written to off once the car's assist service is ready after each power-on, then read back to confirm. Only applied while the car is stationary.
Lane departure assist mode off at every ignition
Sets the lane departure assist mode (the car calls it LKA in its own dialog) to off after each power-on and reads it back to confirm. The car resets this mode at every power cycle, so the write repeats each ignition. Only applied while stationary.
Emergency lane keeping assist off at every ignition
Turns emergency lane keeping assist (ELKA) off after each power-on and reads it back to confirm. ELKA can steer the car; disable it only if you accept that. Only applied while stationary.
Driver monitoring (Cabin Perception) off at every ignition
Asks the car's settings service to turn the driver monitoring switches (monitoring, fatigue, attention) off after each power-on. This route has no readback, so the status can only say the request was sent; check Settings › Cabin Perception on the car.
Available only in DiLink 5 mode with the vehicle model set to Sealion 7 in Settings.

Low-power mode while parked
Saves battery while parked by running the camera at a low frame rate (about 5 fps) when nothing is happening, then instantly ramping back to full quality the moment motion is detected. Motion and object detection are unaffected — the same events are caught — but the recorded footage during quiet periods is choppier to save power. Takes effect while the car is parked and surveillance is armed.

Shadow Filter
Filters out tree shadows, cloud shadows, and other lighting changes that cause false recordings
Normal works well for most outdoor parking. Use Aggressive if parked under trees with heavy shadow movement.
Record on strong motion
Trigger on large, close, moving objects even when the AI can't identify them
Catches events the AI misses — someone walking right up to the car in poor light, or a large vehicle pulling in close, where object recognition returns nothing. Only fires when the movement is big, close, and travels steadily across the scene for about a second, so wind-blown branches, shadows, rain and headlight sweeps are still ignored. Expect some extra recordings with no identified person or vehicle: these are kept even with "Discard non-actor recordings" on, because a clip the AI couldn't classify is the last one worth deleting automatically.
Discard non-actor recordings
Aggressively drop recordings where no real person or vehicle was tracked
Good if you get false alerts from shadows or tree-branch movement — these clips are deleted automatically when nothing real was tracked. Caution: in low light or unclear conditions it can occasionally discard a real threat the AI failed to identify. Leave off if you'd rather keep every recording.
Also discard at night
Extend the filter to dark scenes using motion shape instead of brightness
By default the filter only runs in daylight, because in the dark the AI can miss a real person entirely. With this on, night clips are discarded only when the motion looks like wind-blown foliage or a lighting change (headlights, reflections) and never translated across the scene like a person would. Higher chance of dropping a real night-time event than the daytime filter — leave off if in doubt.
