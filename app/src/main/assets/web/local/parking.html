Enable Parking Intelligence
Opens a session at every switch-off: location and GPS freshness, four-camera stills when you walk away and when you return, the vehicles that arrived or left next to you while sentry watched, and two notifications ("Parked", "Back at car"). Off = no threads, no store, no compute.
Session ends
When a parking session closes. "When I return": first door open or unlock (or power-on). "When the car powers on": door and unlock only refresh the returned stills. "When I drive away": waits until the gear leaves P; without a usable gear reading it closes ~2 minutes after power-on.
Arrived / returned stills
Four-camera stills ~90 s after switch-off and at the first door open or unlock. Also the image on the notifications.
Neighbour timeline
Track vehicles that arrive or leave next to you from the sentry baseline and finalized events, and keep the sharpest frames of each. Needs sentry armed.
Read garage signage (v2)
Read level / zone / bay text from the stills and the last minute of the approach clip with the bundled on-device OCR (PP-OCRv3, ~5.5 MB). Runs once right after the arrival stills so the Parked message carries the level; a read deferred by a recording sentry clip is retried later. Language-specific models can be side-loaded to Overdrive/models/.
