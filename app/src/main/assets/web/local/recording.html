Recording Layout
Camera Layout Applies live
Standard records the four 360 cameras as a 2×2 grid. Dashcam puts the forward road view across the top with the 360 left, rear and right cameras along the bottom. Telemetry overlay is preserved either way.
Use Windshield Camera in Dashcam Layout
When available, use the dedicated windshield camera for the top dashcam view. Falls back to the 360 front camera if the stream cannot be opened.
Place Tagging
Tag dashcam recordings with place names
Reverse-geocode the recording-start GPS into a district/city label and save it next to each clip's sidecar. Off by default.
Use online resolver
Falls back to OpenStreetMap Nominatim when the on-device geocoder cannot name a location. Sends only the recording-start coordinates and your device language.
Custom Nominatim URL
Optional. Point to a self-hosted instance to skip the public OSM endpoint entirely. Shared with surveillance.

Clip Duration Next clip
Length of each recorded video segment. Applies to both driving (ACC on) and parked surveillance (ACC off) recordings, on the next segment rotation.
Longer clips mean fewer, larger files — but an in-progress clip is only finalized when it rotates. On sudden power loss (e.g. battery cut on impact) the current clip can be lost, so a 10-minute setting risks up to 10 minutes of unsaved footage vs 2. Keep 2 min if your vehicle can lose power abruptly.
