Tunnel configuration
Connects the car to your home network through WireGuard. Addresses in AllowedIPs (for example your Home Assistant broker) are reached through the tunnel; everything else keeps its normal route. Upload or paste the peer (client) config your router exported, for example from OPNsense, Fritz!Box or wg-easy.
The private key is stored on the car only and is never shown again. Saving a new config replaces the old one.
A saved config applies immediately while the tunnel is running. Otherwise, switch on "WireGuard Tunnel" in the car's Daemons screen.
