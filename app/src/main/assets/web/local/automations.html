Driving safety guards
These guards apply to matching vehicle actions started through OverDrive's Automations, Action Groups, Key Mapping, Quick Controls, MQTT, Vehicle Controls, and saved seat positions. Turning one off lets OverDrive attempt that action while the car is moving or its state is unknown. Advanced raw shell, CAN, and diagnostic actions are outside these guards.
